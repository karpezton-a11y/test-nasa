## Slide 4 — Why rainfall and AOD

Rainfall measures water reaching the surface. AOD measures light attenuation by particles through the atmospheric column. Their seasonal patterns offer useful environmental questions, but associations require care.

Say: “Our observed rainfall climatology peaks in July in the IGP box and November in the TN box. The two boxes have different seasonal structures. AOD measures the atmospheric column; it does not directly measure surface PM2.5.”

## Slide 9 — Scientific limitations

Cloud-screened AOD, humidity, different resolutions and included grid centres, approximate boxes and observing-system changes limit interpretation. Regional rainfall means smooth local extremes. Threshold-defined anomalies are not causal or trend significance findings.

Say: “Our data checks also record daily–monthly rainfall differences: daily sums are 1.83% lower overall in IGP and 0.97% lower in TN than the separate monthly product. We retain both sources and the exact differences for review. Aadarsh’s independent statistical validation is still required.”
