Why you can trust it

Every result is tested, cross-checked and labelled.

Validated

Our trend engine matches SciPy and pymannkendall on the same data.

665 tests

Automated tests run on every change, on Python 3.11 and 3.13.

Many-test check

Benjamini–Hochberg q-values stop lucky results when scanning many regions.

Gaps stay gaps

Missing months are never filled in; low-coverage months are flagged.

AI can't change numbers

Explanations are checked by code; any changed number is rejected.

Patterns, not causes

Clear caveats: AOD is not PM2.5, and a trend is not a cause.
