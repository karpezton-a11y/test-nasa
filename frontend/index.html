How it works

From a question to a tested answer.

?

Ask

Pick a variable and a place, or start from a plain question like “is my city's air getting worse?”

⇣

Fetch

Real monthly NASA data: MODIS, OMI, GPM IMERG, MERRA-2, NASA POWER. Gaps stay gaps.

∿

De-season

Remove the yearly cycle, so a monsoon or a fire season can't fake a trend.

∑

Test

Mann–Kendall (corrected for autocorrelation), Theil–Sen slope with its range, OLS check, FDR across regions.

◍

Map

See where it rises, falls or stays flat, on the globe and on the map.

✦

Explain

AI rewrites the computed numbers in plain words, and code checks it changed none of them.

Impact

Turns “I heard it's getting worse” into evidence.

Why you can trust it

Every result is tested, cross-checked and labelled.

Validated

Our trend engine matches SciPy and pymannkendall on the same data.

642 tests

Automated tests run on every change, on Python 3.11 and 3.13.

Many-test check

Benjamini–Hochberg q-values stop lucky results when scanning many regions.

Gaps stay gaps

Missing months are never filled in; low-coverage months are flagged.

AI can't change numbers

Explanations are checked by code; any changed number is rejected.

Patterns, not causes

Clear caveats: AOD is not PM2.5, and a trend is not a cause.
